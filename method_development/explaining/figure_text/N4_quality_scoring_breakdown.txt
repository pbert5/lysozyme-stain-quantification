Candidate regions are scored by shape and signal properties, then ranked so lower total score indicates a better crypt match.

- circularity: 0.15
- area: 0.25
- line_fit: 0.35
- red_intensity: 0.85

Effective-count weights:
- circularity: 0.35
- area: 0.15
- line_fit: 0.45
